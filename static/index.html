Important information

Stop positions are approximate, and dashed connections are illustrative model links, not official kombi lines or road directions. Route distances, fares, and travel times are estimates, not live transit information. Current USD fare amounts are not verified prices from local operators. No current Gweru fare schedule with route details, a named source, and a clear effective date has been verified for these estimates. Confirm prices locally before travel.

A user reported a US$0.50 fare for the MSU Main Campus to Gweru CBD trip on 28 September 2026; this report is not independently verified and has no confirmed effective date. The graph applies this reported fare in both directions because its connections are undirected. The Herald reported a general Gweru private kombi range of US$0.75 to US$1 per trip on 24 March 2026, but gave no route schedule or effective date. The general range is context only and is not used for route weights.
